What I've found on real websites.

Reported. Never fixed. Later, exploited.

01 · Educational institution
I found an XSS vulnerability on their site and reported it with steps to fix it. Nobody acted on it.
What happened
Some time later, attackers used that same flaw to inject ads shown to every visitor.
Why I tell this story
It's why retesting is included in every engagement. A report nobody verifies protects no one.
  1. 02

    In through the front door

    Field survey company

    The login had no attempt limit, so a dictionary attack got me in. The password reset code could also be brute-forced.

    On top of that: every reset request cost money, so an attacker could run up their bill (denial of wallet).

    • Authentication
    • Brute force
    • Denial of wallet
  2. 03

    The form that was losing leads

    Real estate developer

    The contact form could be flooded until it went down (application-layer denial of service).

    On top of that: it wasn't sending any email to prospective buyers. Leads were being lost and nobody had noticed.

    • Layer 7 DoS
    • Forms
  3. 04

    A checkout used to test stolen cards

    Online store

    The payment flow allowed card testing (carding): validating stolen or generated card numbers in bulk.

    The risk: chargebacks, fees and the payment processor freezing the account.

    • Payments
    • Carding

Better to show up here as a fixed finding than in the news.
